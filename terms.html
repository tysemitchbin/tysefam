Who it's for

Tyse Fam is a private, non-commercial website for one family. Only people on the family's guest list can sign in and use it. It isn't offered to the public and nothing is sold.

The Energy page and your bank

Connecting a bank account is optional. You can only connect your own account, by approving it yourself with BankID. The connection is read-only and is used only to show the family's power bills; see the privacy page for exactly what is kept. You can disconnect it at any time.

No guarantees

Bill amounts, estimates and prices are shown to help the family keep track. They come from the banks, power companies and public price lists and can be late, missing or wrong. Always check the real invoice before relying on a number.

Changes

These terms may change as the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
