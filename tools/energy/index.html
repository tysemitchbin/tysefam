🏦 Bank: bills fill themselves in

Once a day, the payments to Tibber, BKK and Elvia are read from your bank (read-only, through Enable Banking), so every bill shows the exact amount paid. Nothing to type in.

  1. Sign in at enablebanking.com (they email you a sign-in link).
  2. Open API applications and add one: choose Production, name it Tyse Fam, and add this redirect URL:
    Press Register. A .pem file downloads: that's the app's private key.
  3. On the new application press Activate by linking accounts and log in to your bank with BankID. Pick the account the power bills are paid from.
  4. Below, paste the application ID (the long code with dashes) and choose the .pem file.

✅ Enable Banking app saved.

🔌 Tibber

  1. Open developer.tibber.com and sign in with your usual Tibber login.
  2. Under Access token, press Create token (tick the boxes it offers) and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and press Connect.

If both houses are on the same Tibber login, one token brings in both. If they're on different logins, connect each one. The token is kept privately in our database and is never shown on the website.

🔌 Elvia (optional)

Makes the Elvia grid bill exact by reading the meter straight from Elvia. On Elvia's Min side, open Tilganger, create a token for målerverdier (meter values) and paste it here. Without it, Tibber's numbers are used instead.
