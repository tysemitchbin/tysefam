Privacy

Tyse Fam is a private website for one family. Only family members on its guest list can sign in. Last updated 10 October 2026.

What the bank connection reads

The Energy page can connect to a family member's own bank account through Enable Banking, with their BankID approval. Access is read-only: the site can't move money or make payments.

Of the transactions it can see, the site keeps only payments to or from the family's power companies (Tibber, BKK and Elvia): the amount, the date and the payment text. Every other transaction is ignored and never stored. It also keeps the bank's name, the account's name and the last four digits of the account number, to show which account is connected.

Where it's kept and who can see it

The data is stored in the family's private database (Supabase, in the EU). Only family members who are signed in can see the bills. Access tokens and keys are stored where only the server can read them, never in the website's code.

Other data

The Energy page also reads electricity prices and usage from Tibber and Elvia, and the family's power invoices from email, for the same purpose: showing the family its power bills.

Nothing is shared or sold

No data is sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used for anything other than the family's own overview.

Stopping and deleting

The bank connection can be disconnected at any time on the Energy page (⚙️ Settings → Disconnect), which also ends the permission at the bank. The bank's permission also runs out by itself after the period the bank allows (usually 180 days). Saved bills can be deleted on the Energy page. For anything else, contact the family member who runs the site.
